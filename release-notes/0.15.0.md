## What's new

- The home answers one question now: your balance, one quiet In and Out line, weekly spending bars with the heaviest week labeled, where the money went, what is coming up, and your newest transactions. No KPI tiles, no chart wall.
- Where the money went is a donut again, with the largest category in coral and the rest in a stone ramp, and the legend beside it.
- Money out reads red and money in green everywhere, and the Pay button is one centered word on its near-black tile.
- A first-run tour walks the real app and explains each screen while the rest dims around it, with a small mascot doing the talking. No taps required.
- The tour remembers what it has taught you, so a new feature adds only its own step, and developer tools can replay the whole tour.
- The Pay step explains the USSD flow plainly: the automation fills the menu, and your PIN is entered in the phone's own dialog. Tripwire never sees or stores it.
- Fresh installs tour over a seeded demo statement so the walkthrough shows real screens, and the demo data is removed when the tour ends.
- Insights pick their month from a calendar sheet at the top of the page, and a "Who paid you most" card names the counterparties behind your incoming money, one tap from the ledger.
- Holding a recurring payment opens its options, edit and delete right at the row, with a two-tap confirm.
- A filtered ledger shows its active filters as chips you can clear in one tap, so a single-category list never reads as broken data.
- Picking a contact fills the phone field only now. The star alone saves or removes a favourite.
- A cancelled USSD payment no longer locks the pay buttons. The run ends when you return to the app, says the outcome is unknown and asks you to check your messages, and a three-minute floor releases anything left hanging.
- Every SIM the phone reports appears in the wallet list. Carriers Tripwire cannot analyse yet, Airtel included, are listed and marked "Not supported yet" instead of silently missing.
- The battery prompt opens Tripwire's own settings page first, where Battery is one tap away.
- Developer mode unlocks with ten taps on the Settings tab, and can replay the tour and test every system-settings jump with the device's real state shown beside each.
- Sheets drag to dismiss with one physics everywhere, dark mode separates surfaces by lightness, and the whole app shares one radius language.
- The sponsor tile is gone from content screens; the donation ask is one footnote in Settings.
- Built on Wails 3.1.0, the stable framework release, and it now targets Android 16 (API 36), which Google Play requires for new updates.
